<div class="app-head"><img src="icon.png" alt=""><div><h1>Fude</h1>Kanji dictionary and handwriting trainer for Android</div></div>

Fude (筆, "brush") looks up kanji from any app, shows how every kanji is written, and
grades your handwriting stroke by stroke.

- **Look up from any app.** Select Japanese text anywhere and Fude shows the word, its
  reading and every kanji in it. Conjugated words are recognised too.
- **Stroke order.** Animated, numbered strokes for more than 6,400 kanji and both kana.
- **Write and get graded.** Fude checks every stroke for shape, direction and order and
  tells you what to fix.
- **Reviews and progress.** Spaced repetition and progress by JLPT level or school grade.
- **Made for tablets and pens.** Two-pane layouts, real-size writing boxes, palm rejection
  and pressure-sensitive ink.
- **Offline and private.** No account, no ads. The optional AI tutor uses your own Claude,
  OpenAI or Gemini key and is the only feature that goes online.
