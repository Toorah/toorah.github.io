<div class="app-head"><img src="icon.svg" alt=""><div><h1>Lokum</h1>Turkish–English dictionary and study app for Android</div></div>

Lokum is an offline Turkish–English dictionary that understands Turkish endings, with
flashcards, quizzes and writing practice to learn the words you look up.

- **Look up any form.** Type or select a word in any app, and Lokum reads it back to its
  dictionary form: *gidemiyorum* is "I can't go", from *gitmek*.
- **Full tables.** Conjugations and declensions for every verb and noun, and every
  grammar term explained in plain words with examples.
- **Study what you need.** Flashcards both ways, quizzes, typing practice for verb forms
  and endings, and short writing exercises.
- **Real sentences.** Example sentences for common words, with the matching word marked.
- **Offline and private.** No account, no ads, no internet access. Sync between devices
  goes through a file in a cloud folder you choose.

Levels follow how often words are used in film and TV subtitles.
