# Lokum privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026. Lokum is made by ATK Interactive.

**Short version: Lokum does not collect, send or share any of your data. It has no
internet access at all.**

## What stays on your device

Everything you do in Lokum (saved words, flashcard schedules, quiz and practice answers,
your writing and its reviews, calendar stamps and settings) is stored only on your
device. Lokum has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not
ask Android for internet access, so it cannot send anything anywhere by itself.

## Text from other apps

When you look a word up from another app (the text-selection menu or Share), Lokum reads
that text on your device to find the word. It is not stored unless you save the word.

## Sync (optional)

If you turn on Sync, Lokum writes your progress to one file in a folder **you** choose,
for example in Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox, through Android's own file picker. The
file goes only where you put it; the storage service you pick handles it under its own
privacy policy. We never receive it. Turn Sync off and delete the file to remove it.

## Android backup

Like most apps, Lokum lets Android include your progress and settings in your device's
backup (for example Google's backup on a new phone). Android and your backup provider
handle this; you can turn it off in your phone's settings. We never receive it.

## Speech

The speaker buttons use your phone's text-to-speech engine (a separate app, usually
Google's), which Lokum asks to read the word aloud. That engine's own privacy policy applies.

## Children

Lokum is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
